# Search Strategy

PROSPERO CRD420261344877

## PubMed/MEDLINE

```
("iron overload"[MeSH] OR "iron overload"[tiab] OR hemochromatosis[MeSH] OR haemochromatosis[tiab] OR hemochromatosis[tiab] OR thalassemia[MeSH] OR thalassaemia[tiab] OR thalassemia[tiab] OR "transfusion-dependent"[tiab] OR "dysmetabolic iron overload"[tiab] OR DIOS[tiab] OR "dysmetabolic hepatosiderosis"[tiab])
AND
(hypogonadism[MeSH] OR hypogonadism[tiab] OR "hypogonadotropic hypogonadism"[tiab] OR testosterone[MeSH] OR testosterone[tiab] OR "Leydig cell"[tiab] OR gonadotropin*[tiab] OR "luteinizing hormone"[tiab] OR "follicle stimulating hormone"[tiab] OR "hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal"[tiab] OR "HPG axis"[tiab])
AND
(male[tiab] OR men[tiab] OR males[tiab] OR "Men"[MeSH])
```

Filters: Humans; English; no date restriction. Supplementary Embase-equivalent search via Europe PMC with equivalent terms. Reference lists of included studies and relevant reviews hand-searched.

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Inclusion | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Population | Males with HH, TDT, or DIOS | Female-only; mixed cohorts without extractable male data |
| Exposure | Documented iron overload (ferritin, transferrin saturation, LIC, or genotype) | No iron burden measure |
| Outcome | Hypogonadism prevalence or quantitative hormonal data (testosterone, LH, FSH) | Qualitative only |
| Design | Cohort, cross-sectional, case-control, case series n >= 10 | Single case reports, editorials, letters |

## Meta-analysis eligibility (subset)

TDT studies with sex-specific or extractable male hypogonadism counts and denominators. Six studies met this criterion (see `data/`).

## Risk of bias and certainty

Adapted NIH Quality Assessment Tool for observational cohort and cross-sectional studies; ROBINS-I principles for confounding and selection domains; GRADE for overall certainty (rated very low to low).
